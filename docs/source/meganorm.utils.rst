ERP support modules
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

``meganorm.utils.erp`` holds ERP settings, event resolution, validation and
shared helpers. ``meganorm.utils.erp_bootstrap`` provides resampling and
uncertainty summaries; the ERP pipeline supplies its IDP extractor.
``meganorm.utils.erp_outputs`` handles saving and loading both original ERP
results and bootstrap summaries. ``meganorm.utils.erp_logging`` handles
progress and timing. Participant and cohort HTML reports are generated by
``meganorm.plots.erp_report``.

For normal use, prefer the entry points in :doc:`meganorm.API` and the
:doc:`erp_quickstart`. Earlier imports from ``meganorm.src.erp_bootstrap``,
``meganorm.utils.erp_bootstrap_outputs`` and ``meganorm.utils.erp_index``
remain available through compatibility modules.
